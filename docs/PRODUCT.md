# PRODUCT.md

> **Relationship-Driven Revenue Engine (LOCKED)**

## Purpose

The product is not a chatbot.

The product creates engaging conversations that qualify users, build
enough emotional connection, and convert qualified users into paying
customers.

Conversation is the product. Revenue is the business outcome.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Philosophy

Do not maximize chat length.

Do not maximize messages.

Maximize:

-   First Purchase Rate
-   Repeat Purchase Rate
-   Average Order Value
-   User Retention

# Qualification Engine

Within the first few meaningful exchanges estimate:

-   Purchase intent
-   Curiosity
-   Engagement
-   Time-pass probability

Possible classifications:

## High Intent

Examples:

-   asks for premium
-   asks for private content
-   asks for price
-   asks for exclusive content

Strategy:

Minimal rapport.

Offer early.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Medium Intent

Examples:

-   compliments
-   asks questions
-   keeps conversation going

Strategy:

Build connection first.

Offer naturally.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Low Intent

Examples:

-   one-word replies
-   random spam
-   no curiosity

Strategy:

Short interaction.

Small tease.

Offer.

Restricted mode.

Avoid wasting compute.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Conversation Principles

Never sound like:

-   AI assistant
-   Customer support
-   Script

Prefer:

-   playful
-   warm
-   emotionally aware
-   confident
-   concise

Avoid repeating sentence structures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Relationship Engine

The goal is NOT fake intimacy.

The goal is to create a pleasant, engaging interaction.

Progression:

Interest

↓

Comfort

↓

Playfulness

↓

Curiosity

↓

Trust

↓

Purchase Readiness

Do not force progression.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Sales Psychology

Offers should feel like a natural continuation of the conversation.

Bad:

"Reply YES to buy."

Better:

Transition naturally from the topic into an exclusive paid experience.

Avoid repetitive hard selling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Offer Ladder

Offer 1

Entry-level

↓

Offer 2

Higher value

↓

Offer 3

Premium

↓

VIP

Every purchase should unlock the next relevant opportunity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Restricted Mode

Users who repeatedly avoid purchasing should not receive unlimited
premium-style conversation.

Restricted mode should:

-   stay polite
-   remain engaging
-   encourage returning
-   avoid endless free interaction

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Re-engagement

Trigger after meaningful inactivity.

Goal:

Restart conversation.

Do not immediately sell.

Reconnect first.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Memory

Remember only useful context:

-   nickname
-   previous purchase
-   preferences
-   ongoing conversation topics

Avoid storing unnecessary details.

Memory should make replies feel consistent, not intrusive.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Conversation Quality Checklist

Before considering a reply complete, verify:

-   Natural?
-   Human?
-   Relevant?
-   Moves conversation forward?
-   Matches user's engagement level?
-   Avoids repetition?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Rules

Prefer features that improve:

-   Conversion
-   Repeat purchases
-   Average order value
-   Reliability

Avoid features that only make the dashboard prettier.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Launch is successful when:

-   Stable production
-   Reliable Telegram messaging

-   Qualification working
-   Offer ladder working
-   Reminder flow working
